## Research summary

This early extended abstract presents a Kabyle spell checker combining edit distance, phonetic matching, n-gram context models and linguistic rules for the state of annexation. It describes community participation and an evaluation using synthetic spelling noise. Evaluation on real errors from OCR and automatic speech recognition is identified as ongoing work; LLM integration is future work in this version.
