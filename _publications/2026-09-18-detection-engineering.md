## Contribution in brief

This study distinguishes an alert that fires from an alert that correctly identifies the emulated ATT&CK technique. Its four outcomes are **det** (correct detection), **det*** (an alert with an incorrect technique mapping), **tel** (telemetry without an alert) and **none** (missing telemetry). The mapping-quality gap is the difference between effective coverage and strict coverage.

The revised presentation describes four scenarios and 80 laboratory runs. Adding 2,396 community rules increases effective coverage by 33.1 percentage points while adding no strict detections in those scenarios. Knowledge-graph diagnosis then guides targeted rule authoring. These targeted rules were evaluated on the same scenarios that informed their design; validation on held-out scenarios remains future work.
